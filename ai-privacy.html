Unknown Unknowns in Claude Code

A series for people who are not computing people. If you hold other people’s data — students, patients, clients, research subjects — and you have started using an AI assistant that runs on your own computer, there are about six risks you probably cannot yet see. Each installment explains one in plain language and ends with something you can do that same day.
