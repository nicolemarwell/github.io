Organizations and the Governance of Urban Poverty

Nicole P. Marwell & Shannon Morrissey

Annual Review of Sociology, 46, 233–250

ArticleInvited Review
DOI ↗

Abstract

Argues that the dominant social-disorganization lens obscures the many ways formal organizations produce and manage urban poverty, and proposes an approach combining urban governance and strategic-action-fields frameworks to open new research directions.
